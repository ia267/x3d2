public pure subroutine cuda_fft_spectral_dims(bc_code, dims_glob, dims_loc, nproc_dir, nrank_dir, n_spec, n_sp_st)

The size n_spec and the offset n_sp_st of the local spectral pencil in the permuted state, for the BC class bc_code on the cell dimensions dims_glob and dims_loc, the decomposition nproc_dir and the rank position nrank_dir. A BC class the solver does not support gives zeros. Shared by the solver init and the x3d2-memcheck estimate.
