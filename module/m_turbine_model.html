turbine_model_t

Abstract interface for turbine forcing models (actuator disc, actuator line)

A turbine model is a backend-agnostic physics object: it samples the flow at actuator points and accumulates a momentum source back onto the RHS fields (du, dv, dw). It is owned and driven entirely by the flow case (see m_case_wind_turbine) and therefore knows nothing about the solver, the case, or the iturbine switch - the case decides which concrete model to allocate and when to call it.

Concrete implementations: - turbine_dummy_t (m_turbine_dummy): no-op / iturbine=0, the fallback - alm_t (m_alm) : actuator line model - adm_t (m_adm) : actuator disc model
